事先
HSK4		shì xiānIn advance, beforehand. 事先确认 = "confirm in advance." Synonym: 提前 (ahead of time, more colloquial). 事先 is slightly more formal; often used in procedures, planning, warnings: 事先说明, 事先通知.
确认
HSK4		què rènTo confirm, verify. 确认一下 = "just confirm/check." 一下 softens the request (see grammar note). Collocations: 确认信息, 确认身份, 请确认. Near-synonym 核实 (verify facts) is more investigative and formal.
进行
HSK3		jìn xíngTo carry out, conduct, engage in. Used with abstract or formal activities: 进行讨论, 进行手术, 进行骚扰. It converts a noun into a verb phrase. The father uses it here satirically formally: 对…进行性骚扰 = "to conduct sexual harassment against."
性骚扰
phrase		xìng sāo rǎoSexual harassment. The father applies this adult legal term absurdly to a 7-year-old's declaration of love — the comedy lies in the mismatch of register. 骚扰 alone = to harass/disturb (can be non-sexual).
才
HSK3		cáiOnly, merely — stresses that a quantity or age is surprisingly small. 才七岁 = "only seven years old!" Contrast 就 (already, stresses earliness): 就七岁了 = "already seven." Same character, opposite implications depending on context.
一下
grammar		yī xiàSoftener after a verb — reduces the formality/imposingness of a request or action. 确认一下 = "just check / take a look." Compare 确认 (blunt/abrupt) vs 确认一下 (casual, polite). Also signals a brief/single action.
