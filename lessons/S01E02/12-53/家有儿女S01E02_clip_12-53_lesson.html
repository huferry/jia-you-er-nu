HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
凭什么
HSK4		píng shén meOn what basis? What right do you have? A sharp rhetorical challenge. 凭 = relying on, based on. 凭什么 = "based on what?" Implies the speaker finds the action unjustified. Very confrontational in tone — here used as a running joke with both sides asking it simultaneously. Compare: 有什么资格 (what qualification do you have).
惩罚
HSK4		chéng fáTo punish; punishment. 惩 = penalize, 罚 = fine/penalize. Collocations: 受到惩罚 (receive punishment), 惩罚机制 (punishment mechanism). Here the whole family debates who deserves punishment, culminating in a logical standoff where nobody can justify anything.
天理何在
idiom		tiān lǐ hé zàiWhere is heaven's justice? A set phrase expressing moral outrage at an injustice. 天理 = natural moral order; 何在 = where is it (literary). Formal/dramatic register — used here by Xiaoyu for comic effect since his grievance is trivial. Compare: 说不过去 (can't justify this).
相声
HSK5		xiàng shengCrosstalk comedy — a traditional Chinese two-person comedic dialogue art form (逗哏 sets up jokes, 捧哏 feeds lines). Here the adults compare their circular argument to a crosstalk routine. Collocations: 说相声 (perform crosstalk), 相声演员 (crosstalk performer). Cultural note: a quintessential Beijing entertainment form.
待会
colloquial		dāi huìIn a little while, in a moment. Spoken contraction of 等一会儿. Note the pronunciation: 待 → dāi (not dài). Very common in Northern Chinese speech. Compare: 一会儿 (a moment), 过一会 (after a bit). Used here by the warm stepmother offering to bring fruit — a small but telling detail of her nurturing character.
弄
HSK3		nòngTo get, fix up, deal with — a highly versatile colloquial verb. 弄点水果 = get/prepare some fruit. 弄 replaces more specific verbs (拿, 准备, 做) in casual speech. Other uses: 弄清楚 (sort out), 弄坏 (break), 弄错 (get wrong). One of the most flexible verbs in spoken Mandarin.
