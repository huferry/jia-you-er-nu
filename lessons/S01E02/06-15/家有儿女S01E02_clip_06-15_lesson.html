HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
站住
colloquial		zhàn zhùStop! Halt! Used as an imperative command to stop someone moving. 住 as a resultative complement = successfully held in place. Compare: 停下 (stop what you're doing), 别动 (don't move). 站住 is specifically for stopping someone who is walking/leaving.
绝不
formal		jué bùAbsolutely not, under no circumstances. 绝 = absolutely/completely. 绝不会允许 = will absolutely not permit. Much stronger than 不会 alone. Used in firm declarations. Compare: 决不 (same meaning, 决 = resolved), 永远不 (never ever). Both 绝不 and 决不 are correct.
允许
HSK4		yǔn xǔTo permit, allow. More formal than 让 (let) or 准 (approve). 我不会允许你们 = "I won't allow you." Collocations: 允许…进入 (permit entry), 经…允许 (with permission of). The double 不会允许 + 绝 stresses the absoluteness of the refusal.
消气
colloquial		xiāo qìTo let anger go, to calm down. 消 = dispel/extinguish; 气 = anger (breath/energy). 消消气 (reduplicated verb) = calm down a little. Similar: 消火 (extinguish the fire [of anger]), 息怒 (formal: suppress anger). Reduplication softens the command.
脾气
HSK3		pí qiTemper, disposition. 脾气不好 = has a bad temper. 发脾气 = to lose one's temper. The 脾 (spleen) in Chinese medicine was associated with emotion regulation — hence this compound. Compare: 性格 (character, more permanent), 情绪 (mood, more temporary).
作为…
grammar		zuò wéi…As / in the capacity of. 作为夏雪的父亲 = "As Xiaoxue's father [I…]" Introduces a role or identity that justifies the action following. More formal than 我是夏雪的父亲，所以…. Used in both formal writing and elevated spoken rhetoric. Compare: 以…身份 (in the capacity of).
