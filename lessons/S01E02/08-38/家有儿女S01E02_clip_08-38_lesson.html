庸俗
HSK5		yōng súVulgar, philistine, lowbrow — lacking refinement or idealism. 庸俗 implies a crass, money-minded mentality. The boy rejects the assumption that money is the motive; the transaction is an intellectual exchange. Near-synonym: 粗俗 (crude/coarse, more about manners); 低俗 (lowbrow, often about content).
金钱交易
HSK4+		jīn qián jiāo yìA financial transaction; a cash deal. 交易 alone = transaction/trade (commercial). The phrase 这不是金钱交易 dismisses the mercenary interpretation. 交易 also appears in stock market context: 证券交易所. Here used somewhat dramatically to elevate what is just tutoring barter.
理科
HSK5		lǐ kēScience subjects (maths, physics, chemistry, biology) — as opposed to 文科 (humanities/arts subjects). The Chinese high-school track system divides students into 理科生 and 文科生. The problem referenced (page 48, problem 3, line 16) is a science problem Xia Xue can solve.
除了…才
grammar		chú le…cái"Only if… then…" — a conditional construction expressing a prerequisite. 除了我演得特别像，她才肯… = "Only if I perform convincingly will she agree to…" Note: 才 here means "only then" (not "just/only" in terms of quantity). Compare: 只有…才 (only by… can…), which is more formal.
肯
HSK4		kěnTo be willing to; to consent to do something (usually a positive willingness). 她才肯跟我讲题 = only then will she be willing to explain the problem. Near-synonym: 愿意 (want/willing, more about desire); 肯 implies deliberate agreement, often after some condition is met.
学会
HSK3		xué huìTo learn how to do something; to master a skill. 只有她学会 = only she has mastered it. The resultative complement 会 (can/know how to) added to 学 shows successful completion of learning. Compare: 学了 (studied it, completed action but result unclear); 学会了 (fully learned/mastered).
