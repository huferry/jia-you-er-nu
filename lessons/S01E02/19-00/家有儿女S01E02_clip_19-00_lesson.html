HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
重复
HSK3		chóng fùTo repeat, say/do again. 重 here is chóng (again/re-), NOT zhòng (heavy/important). Contrast: 重要 (zhòng yào, important), 重复 (chóng fù, repeat). Very common: 重复一遍 = "say it one more time."
鸡毛换钻石戒指
phrase		jī máo huàn zuàn shí jiè zhiExchange a chicken feather for a diamond ring. This absurd promise is 小雨's childish romantic gesture — giving 多多 a chicken feather and vowing to exchange it for a ring when he grows up. 换 = to swap/exchange; 戒指 = ring (finger ring).
当真
phrase		dàng zhēnTo take seriously, treat as real. 不能当真 = "you can't take it at face value." Note 当 here is dàng (to treat as / regard as), NOT dāng (to be / when). Contrast: 认真 (take seriously, do carefully) — 当真 implies credulity, 认真 implies diligence.
小花花公子
colloquial		xiǎo huā huā gōng zǐLittle playboy / young lothario. 花花公子 (huāhua gōngzǐ) = Playboy — a womanizer, flirt, dandy. Adding 小 makes it contemptuous and comic: "this little two-timing kid." The father uses it to condemn 小雨's multiple romantic ventures.
到底
HSK3		dào dǐOn earth, after all, in the end — adds urgency or exasperation to a question. 你到底管还是不管？ = "Are you going to handle this or NOT?" Interchangeable in questions with 究竟 (more literary). Position: before the verb.
难道
HSK4		nán dàoDon't tell me…? Surely not…? A rhetorical question marker implying the speaker finds the implied answer absurd. 难道是我自己想象出来的？ = "Are you seriously suggesting I made it up?" Always forms a rhetorical question; answer expected: "of course not."
