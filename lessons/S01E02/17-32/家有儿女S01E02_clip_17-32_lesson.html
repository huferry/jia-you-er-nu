HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
肯定
HSK3		kěn dìngCertainly, definitely; also "to affirm/confirm." As an adverb: 肯定 + V = "definitely does/did." Here: 肯定忘拿钥匙了 = "He definitely forgot his key." Very common in spoken Chinese for confident inference. Near-synonym: 一定 (more literary/formal); 肯定 is more colloquial.
事关重大
idiom		shì guān zhòng dàThe matter is of great importance / the stakes are high. A formal fixed phrase: 事 (matter) + 关 (concerns/involves) + 重大 (significant). Note 重 here is zhòng (weighty), not chóng. Used to justify formal or dramatic behavior — here the neighbor uses it to request the "man of the house."
男主人
phrase		nán zhǔ rénThe man of the house, male head of household. The counterpart is 女主人 (mistress of the house). The neighbor's insistence on speaking only to the 男主人 is played for comedy — the women are clearly equally (or more) in charge.
由于
HSK4		yóu yúBecause of, due to — introduces a cause, more formal than 因为. 由于…, … = "Because of …, [therefore] …." 由于事关重大 = "Given that the matter is serious." Common in written Chinese and formal speech; 因为 is more colloquial.
坐下
HSK2		zuò xiaSit down. 坐下谈话 = "sit down and talk." Directional complement: 坐 (sit) + 下 (down). The polite formula 要不然坐下谈话 softens the confrontation — "why not just sit and talk it over?"
楼上
HSK3		lóu shàngUpstairs; "the floor above." 就住在你家楼上 = "I live just upstairs from you." 就 adds "right/exactly" — the neighbor is a close physical neighbor as well as an intrusive one. Contrast 楼下 (downstairs).
