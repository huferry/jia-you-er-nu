狂野
colloquial		kuángyěWild, feral, unrestrained. Used here by young boys as aspirational cool-speak. Collocation: 狂野的个性 (wild personality), 狂野西部 (Wild West). Softer near-synonym: 野 alone; stronger: 疯狂.
决斗
HSK5+		jué dòuA formal duel; loosely any decisive face-off. 为…决斗 = duel for someone. The classical romance trope — fighting for a girl's honor — here used humorously by kids who've been reading too many adventure novels.
誓言
HSK5		shì yánOath, solemn vow. 爱情誓言 = love pledge. More dramatic/literary than 保证 (guarantee) or 承诺 (promise). The joke: the boys found it in a novel (page 48, some line) and are reciting it like a script.
反正
HSK4		fǎn zhèngAnyway, in any case — dismisses prior objections and asserts a fixed conclusion. 反正…的 = "[it] will… regardless." Compare: 总之 (in short, more formal/written). Very common in spoken argument and confident declarations.
保护
HSK3		bǎo hùTo protect, guard. Core HSK3 word. 会保护你的 uses 会…的 for confident reassurance (see below). Collocations: 保护环境, 保护隐私, 保护动物.
会…的
grammar		huì…deConfident future assertion or reassurance. 的 at the end adds certainty: 我们会保护你的 = "We WILL protect you." vs plain 我们会保护你. Reappears in line 11: 我们以后也会对女孩子说的. Very common in comforting, bragging, or determined statements.
