只许州官放火，不许百姓点灯
idiom		zhǐ xǔ zhōuguān fànghuǒ, bù xǔ bǎixìng diǎndēngOfficials may set fires; commoners may not even light lamps. A classical chéngyǔ-style saying for double standards — one rule for the powerful, another for everyone else. 小雨 rephrases it comically as "只许姐姐放火，不许弟弟点灯."
反抗
HSK4		fǎn kàngTo resist, rebel, fight back. Stronger than 反对 (oppose/object); 反抗 implies active resistance. 反 (reverse) + 抗 (resist/withstand). Common in political and personal contexts: 反抗压迫, 反抗父母.
反正
HSK4		fǎn zhèngAnyway, regardless — signals that the speaker dismisses prior complications and states a fixed conclusion. 反正…也 = "…anyway/either way." Very common in spoken Chinese for brushing off objections with confidence.
惩罚
HSK4		chéng fáPunishment, penalty. 受惩罚 = to receive/suffer punishment; 不受惩罚 = to go unpunished. Near-synonym 处罚 is more administrative/formal; 惩罚 can be personal and moral. Collocation: 严厉的惩罚 (severe punishment).
保持沉默
phrase		bǎo chí chén mòTo remain silent, to keep quiet. 我有权保持沉默 echoes the Miranda right ("right to remain silent") — here used humorously by 小雨 to dodge questions. 保持 = maintain/keep; 沉默 = silence. Contrast: 开口 (to open one's mouth/speak up).
有权
HSK4		yǒu quánTo have the right to (do something). 有权 + V = "have the right to V." More assertive than 可以. Here used ironically by a child invoking legal-sounding language in a family squabble.
