阴影
HSK5		yīn yǐngShadow; psychological shadow/trauma. 离婚阴影 = the psychological shadow of [a parent's] divorce. Literal: shadow cast by an object. Figurative: lasting negative psychological impact. Compare: 创伤 (trauma, more clinical), 心理阴影 (psychological shadow, more explicit).
清醒
HSK5		qīng xǐngTo be clear-headed, to come to one's senses; to recover from a shock. 从…清醒过来 = to snap out of / recover from [something]. Also means to wake up mentally. Compare: 醒来 (to wake up physically), 振作 (to pull oneself together).
幼小
HSK5+		yòu xiǎoYoung and tender/small; used of children or immature things. 幼小心灵 (young tender heart/mind) is a set phrase often used semi-dramatically. Collocation: 幼小的孩子, 幼小的心灵. Near-synonym: 年幼 (young in age, more neutral/formal).
就许…不许…
grammar		jiù xǔ…bù xǔ…Double standard construction: "You're allowed to… but [I'm] not allowed to…" 就许你结婚离婚再结婚，不许我交男朋友 = "You can marry/divorce/remarry, but I can't even date." 就 emphasizes the unfairness. Related idiom: 只许州官放火，不许百姓点灯.
干嘛
colloquial		gàn máWhat are you doing? / What's the matter? Note 干 = gàn here (4th tone), not gān (dry). 干嘛呀 = casual "what's going on?" or mild scolding "what are you doing?!" Very common in Beijing-flavored speech. More casual than 干什么 or 做什么.
从…过来
grammar		cóng…guò lái从 [bad situation] 过来 = to come through/recover from something. 从你的离婚阴影中清醒过来 = recover/snap out of the shadow of your divorce. 过来 as a directional complement here means "come through to this side" = fully recover. Compare: 走出来 (walk out of / get over).
