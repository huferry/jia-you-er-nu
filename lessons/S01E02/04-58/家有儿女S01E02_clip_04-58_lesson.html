稀里糊涂
colloquial		xī li hú tuIn a muddled, confused, half-aware state. A four-character colloquial phrase (not a 成语 but fixed expression). 稀里糊涂的就… = "in a daze, just went and…" Conveys acting without thinking clearly. Compare: 糊里糊涂 (same meaning), 迷迷糊糊 (half-asleep/dazed).
心血来潮
idiom		xīn xuè lái cháoOn a sudden whim, struck by an impulse. Lit. "blood rushes to the heart." 成语. 什么时候心血来潮 = "whenever he gets a sudden whim." Usually mildly pejorative — implies impulsive, unplanned action. Compare: 一时兴起 (moment of excitement), 临时起意 (a sudden idea).
轰出去
colloquial		hōng chū qùTo drive/chase someone out loudly; to boot someone out. 轰 = to shout/drive away (like shooing animals). 把他轰出去 = boot him out. More forceful and rude than 请他走 (ask him to leave) or 叫他出去. Implies rudeness not acceptable in the situation.
正面冲突
HSK5+		zhèng miàn chōng tūHead-on confrontation, direct clash. 正面 = frontal, direct; 冲突 = conflict. 发生正面冲突 = to have a direct confrontation. Formal-sounding phrase used in contexts from family drama to international relations. Compare: 闹翻 (fall out badly), 吵架 (quarrel).
智囊团
colloquial		zhì náng tuánThink tank; brain trust. Lit. "wisdom sack group." 智囊 = advisor/brains (single person), 团 = group. Here used humorously — the stepmother is calling her new husband her personal think tank. Compare: 参谋 (advisor), 出谋划策 (to devise strategies).
非得…不可
grammar		fēi děi…bù kěMust absolutely, have to without exception. 非 (fēi) + 得 (děi) = must/have to (emphatic). 你别非得跟他发生冲突 = "Don't insist on having a clash with him." 非得 alone already means "must"; 不可 at the end makes it complete. Compare: 一定要 (must), 必须 (must, formal).
