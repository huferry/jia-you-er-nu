HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
文弱书生
idiom		wén ruò shū shēngA pale, frail scholar; a bookish young man with no physical prowess. The stereotype of someone who reads all day but can't fight. Opposite of 武将 (warrior). Used here to mock the boy: how can a "文弱书生" call himself "Wild Boy"?
为…着急
HSK4+		wèi…zháojíTo worry about / be anxious for someone. 着急 = to feel anxious/rushed. The 为 marks the person you worry *for* (不为自己着急 = not worried about oneself). Compare: 担心 (concerned), 焦虑 (anxious/nervous, more formal).
负责
HSK4		fù zéTo be responsible for; to take responsibility. 为你们负责 = acting responsibly on your behalf. Can precede 任 for extra formality: 负责任. Contrast: 不负责任 (irresponsible) — appears later in the episode.
积累
HSK5		jī lěiTo accumulate, build up gradually. 积累知识 = accumulate knowledge. Implies a slow, sustained process. Collocations: 积累经验 (gain experience), 积累财富 (build wealth). Near-synonym 积攒 emphasizes saving up, more colloquial.
把精力放在…上
grammar		bǎ jīnglì fàng zài…shàng"Put one's energy/attention into…" The 把-construction moves the object (精力) before the verb for emphasis. 精力 = mental and physical energy. Collocation: 把时间放在…上 (spend time on). Common in exhortations about study or work.
谈恋爱
HSK4		tán liàn'àiTo date someone; to be in a romantic relationship. 谈 here means "to engage in." Note: 谈恋爱 is the process (dating), while 恋爱 alone is the state/feeling (love/romance). 早恋 (premature romance) = dating while underage — a key word in this episode.
