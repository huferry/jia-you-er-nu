WordPinyinExplanation
摔
HSK4		shuāiTo smash/throw/drop (something) down hard. 摔了它 = smash it. 摔 implies a forceful throw downward, often out of anger. Compare: 砸 (smash by striking), 扔 (throw away). The substitution of a plastic glass here is a classic calming gesture.
过瘾
colloquial		guò yǐnSatisfying, to really scratch the itch — to fully indulge a craving or desire. 摔得过瘾 = satisfying to smash. 过 = pass through; 瘾 = addiction/craving. Very colloquial. Compare: 爽 (feels great), 痛快 (exhilarating). Often used for physical satisfaction.
拧着来
colloquial		nǐng zhe láiTo stubbornly go against, to do the opposite out of defiance. 拧 (nǐng) = to be contrary/obstinate. 跟你拧着来 = dig in against you. This is a key parenting insight in the scene: opposition breeds rebellion. Compare: 对着干 (deliberately oppose), 较劲 (lock horns).
着 (zhe)
grammar		zhe着 as a durative/manner suffix: marks ongoing state or manner. 拧着来 = come at it in a stubborn manner. Distinct from 着 (zháo, to touch/achieve) and 着 (zhāo, a move in chess). In 看着/说着/跟着, 着 = zhe (neutral tone), indicating sustained action.
越…也…
grammar		yuè…yě…The more… the more… (asymmetric variant). Standard pattern: 越…越…; here 越反对…也跟你拧着来 uses 也 instead of the second 越 for a slightly more colloquial feel: "the more [you] oppose, [they] also push back." Compare: 越来越 (more and more over time).
消火
colloquial		xiāo huǒTo cool down anger; lit. extinguish the fire. 摆摆火 (here transcribed) likely = 消消火 = calm the anger a bit. 火 metaphorically = anger (怒火). Related: 发火 (to flare up in anger), 火气 (angry energy), 消气 (let the anger go). Compare: 冷静 (calm down, more neutral).
