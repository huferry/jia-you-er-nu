只许州官放火，不许百姓点灯
chengyu		zhǐ xǔ zhōu guān fàng huǒ, bù xǔ bǎi xìng diǎn dēngOfficials may set fires; common people may not even light lamps. A classical idiom (成语) from the Song Dynasty about hypocrisy and double standards in power. Here Liu Xing literally acts it out with a candle to make his point about unfair treatment. The episode then remixes it as: 只许姐姐放火，不许弟弟点灯. Collocations: 这不是只许…不许… (isn't this a case of…?).
成语
HSK4		chéng yǔFour-character set phrase / idiom. 成 = established, 语 = language. Chinese has thousands of these, usually from classical texts or historical anecdotes. They compress a whole story into 4 characters. 记住这个成语 = "remember this idiom." Liu Xing shows off by citing a real 成语 to win an argument — a clever rhetorical move.
州官
literary		zhōu guānA prefectural official (historical). In ancient China, 州 was an administrative region; 官 = official. The idiom contrasts 州官 (privileged authority) with 百姓 (ordinary people). Today used only in this idiom and similar contexts — not an active word. The power hierarchy it encodes remains culturally resonant.
百姓
HSK4		bǎi xìngCommon people, ordinary citizens. 百 = hundred (metaphor for "all"), 姓 = surname. Literally "the hundred surnames" — meaning all the common folk. Formal/literary feel; in speech, 老百姓 (old hundred-surnames) is more common and affectionate. Compare: 人民 (the people, political register), 市民 (city residents).
只许…不许…
grammar		zhǐ xǔ…bù xǔ…Allowed only for X but not for Y — a pattern expressing double standards. 只许 = only permit; 不许 = do not permit. The pattern can be extended: 只许你…不许我… (only you are allowed…not me…). Very useful for expressing unfairness or irony. 只 = only (restrictive); compare 仅 (only, more formal/written).
放火
HSK4+		fàng huǒTo set fire, to commit arson. 放 = release/set, 火 = fire. Here used both literally (Liu Xing lights a candle dramatically) and figuratively (Xiaoxue making trouble). Collocations: 放火烧 (set fire to and burn), 放火犯 (arsonist). Contrast: 点灯 (light a lamp) — the two halves of the idiom frame the power imbalance perfectly.
