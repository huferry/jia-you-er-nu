HSK 3+ Vocabulary

WordPinyinExplanation
约会
HSK4		yuē huìTo go on a date; a date, an appointment. 约会 implies a romantic or social meeting arranged in advance. Here used by Liu Xing to argue for equal treatment: if his sister can date, why can't he? Collocations: 约会对象 (date/partner), 赴约 (keep an appointment). Note: 约 alone can mean "arrange to meet" in a broader sense.
野蛮
HSK5		yě mánSavage, barbaric, rough. Originally: uncivilized/primitive (野蛮人 = savage). Here used as a playful label for a rough-and-tumble girlfriend — a pop-culture reference to the 2001 Korean film 엽기적인 그녀 (野蛮女友 in Chinese). The word has been domesticated into affectionate slang for a feisty girl. Contrast: 文明 (civilized).
突然
HSK3		tū ránSuddenly, all of a sudden. 会突然发现 = will suddenly find out. 突然 as an adverb precedes the verb. As an adjective: 突然的变化 (sudden change). Compare: 忽然 (suddenly — more literary and for internal/mental shifts), 猛然 (suddenly — more physical/violent). 突然 is the most common spoken form.
不公平
HSK3		bù gōng píngUnfair, unjust. 公平 = fair, equal. Liu Xing's repeated cry of 不公平 is both his genuine complaint and a comic refrain. Note 不 sandhi: before 公 (1st tone) it stays bù. Collocations: 对…不公平 (unfair to...), 不公平竞争 (unfair competition). The double standard — one rule for sister, another for brother — is the episode's central theme.
要是…就…
grammar		yào shì…jiù…Conditional pattern: "If… then…". 要是 is a spoken variant of 如果/假如. 你要是敢有野蛮女友，你就会突然发现… = "If you dare to have a Savage Girlfriend, then you'll suddenly discover…" The 就 in the result clause is mandatory in this pattern. Very common in conversational threats, warnings, and bargains.
闭嘴
colloquial		bì zuǐShut up! Close one's mouth. 闭 = close, 嘴 = mouth. Direct and rude when aimed at someone; here Liu Xing says "我跟她闭嘴" meaning he needs to make his mom stop talking — using it about a third person is slightly softer. Collocations: 叫他闭嘴 (tell him to shut up). Compare: 别说了 (stop talking, softer).
