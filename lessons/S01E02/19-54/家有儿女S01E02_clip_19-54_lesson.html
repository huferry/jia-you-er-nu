国情
HSK5		guó qíngNational conditions, the state of a country / its social norms and realities. 中国国情 = "the Chinese context/norms." A common phrase in political and social discourse, often used to explain why something that works elsewhere doesn't apply to China. Here used humorously.
不像话
colloquial		bù xiàng huàOutrageous, absurd, out of line. Literally "doesn't resemble speech/words" → too improper to even describe. 这太不像话了 = "this is simply unacceptable." Colloquial; near-synonym 太过分了 (too much/excessive) is used moments later in the same scene.
不得不
grammar		bù dé bù"Have no choice but to…" — a double-negative structure meaning compulsion without alternatives. 不得不 + V = "must V whether one likes it or not." Near-synonym: 只好 (can only…, used resignedly). 不得不 is slightly more formal/dramatic.

formal		yán jiā guǎn jiàoTo discipline strictly, to bring up with rigorous supervision. 严 (strict) + 加 (apply/increase) + 管教 (discipline/raise). A formal phrase used in letters, official complaints, or scoldings. The father uses it here as a parting shot to the family.
过分
HSK4		guò fènExcessive, going too far, out of line. 太过分了 = "that's way too much!" A very common spoken expression of reproach. 过分 can be used as adjective or predicate. Near-synonym: 过头 (gone too far, more colloquial); 不像话 (outrageous, same register).
尽快
HSK3		jǐn kuàiAs quickly as possible, at the earliest opportunity. Note: 尽 here is jǐn (to the utmost), NOT jìn (to exhaust/finish). Common in instructions and promises: 我会尽快处理 = "I'll deal with it ASAP." Near-synonym: 赶快 (hurry up — imperative urging); 尽快 is more polite/formal.
