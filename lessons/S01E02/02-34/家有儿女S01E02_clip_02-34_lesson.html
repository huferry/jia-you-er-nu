HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
凭什么
colloquial		píng shén meWhat right do you have? On what basis? A strong challenge phrase. 你凭什么…= "By what right do you…?" Much more confrontational than 为什么 (why). Common in arguments between equals or when someone feels unjustly restricted. Tone: indignant.
干涉
HSK5		gān shèTo interfere, meddle. 干涉 implies unwanted intrusion into someone's affairs. Note: 干 here is gān (1st tone), not gàn (do/work). Compare: 插手 (to stick one's hand in), 过问 (to inquire/involve oneself). Collocations: 干涉内政, 干涉私事.
社交
HSK5		shè jiāoSocial interaction, socializing. 社交生活 = social life. Used somewhat formally here — Xiaoxue uses it deliberately to sound grown-up and push back. Compare: 交际 (social dealings, more formal/business). Modern usage: 社交媒体 (social media).
犯错误
HSK4		fàn cuò wùTo make a mistake, commit an error. 犯 = to commit/violate. 犯错误 is slightly more serious than 出错 (make an error) — implies moral or behavioral failing. The parent's anxiety here is realistic: 犯错误 echoes political-era language of "making mistakes."
从来都没有
grammar		cóng lái dōu méi yǒuNever have, not once. 从来 = from always/ever; 都 = even/all; 没有 = have not. This triple-negation structure is emphatic: 你从来都没有关心过我 = "You have NEVER once cared about me." 过 adds the experiential aspect. Contrast: 一直没有 (continuously haven't).
怎么说话的
colloquial		zěn me shuō huà deWhat kind of way is that to talk?! A rhetorical scolding formula. The 的 at the end creates an implied judgment. Used by adults when a child says something shockingly disrespectful. Equivalent register: "Watch your mouth!" Compare: 你说什么 (what did you say? — simpler surprise).
