HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
大半夜
colloquial		dà bàn yèThe middle of the night, deep in the night. A spoken intensifier: 大 amplifies 半夜 (midnight). "What are you doing 大半夜?" implies it's outrageous to be this loud this late. Compare: 深夜 (deep night, more literary/formal), 半夜三更 (the dead of night, 4-character set phrase). Common in scolding.
商量
HSK3		shāng liángTo discuss, consult. 跟…商量件事 = discuss something with someone. Implies a two-way conversation seeking agreement, softer than 要求 (demand) or 通知 (notify). Note: 商量 requires a person object (跟你商量), unlike 讨论 which can take a topic (讨论这个问题). Here Liu Xing uses it strategically to soften an outrageous request.
资金
HSK5		zī jīnFunds, capital. A formal/business word — hilariously misused by the boy to ask his mom for pocket money to dye his hair. The register gap (formal financial term for a child's whim) is the joke. Normal spoken alternative: 钱 (money) or 零用钱 (pocket money). Collocations: 资金不足 (insufficient funds), 筹集资金 (raise funds).
染
HSK5		rǎnTo dye, to color; also to infect/taint. 染成绿的 = dye it green. 染头发 = dye hair. The same character appears earlier in 染上了整个沙漠 (spread across the desert) — a clever echo: the song's "love spreading like fire" and the boy's literal request to "dye" something both use 染.
玩意
colloquial		wán yìThing, stuff, gadget — informal and slightly dismissive. 这玩意 = this thing. Used here by Liu Xing about his hair (!) — calling his own hair "this thing" shows casual self-mockery. Compare: 东西 (thing, neutral), 玩意儿 (more Beijing-accented, with 儿化). Avoid in formal writing.
闹
HSK3		nàoTo make noise, cause trouble, kick up a fuss. 又闹上来 = started making a ruckus again. 上来 as directional complement here means the noise/trouble has resumed/risen. Collocations: 闹笑话 (make a laughingstock of oneself), 闹情绪 (throw a tantrum), 闹钟 (alarm clock — the clock that "makes noise"). Very expressive verb.
