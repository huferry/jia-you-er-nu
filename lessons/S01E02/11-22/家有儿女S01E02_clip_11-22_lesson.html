WordPinyinExplanation
干涉
HSK4+		gān shèTo interfere, meddle. 不再干涉 = won't interfere anymore. Note: 干 here is gān (the intervene/meddle reading), not gàn (do/work). Compare: 干扰 (disrupt/interfere with a signal); 插手 (stick one's hand in, informal meddling). 干涉 is more forceful/formal than 插手.
综合素质
HSK5+		zōng hé sù zhìOverall/comprehensive qualities, all-round capability. 综合 = comprehensive, combined; 素质 = innate + cultivated qualities, character, capability. Common in Chinese education/HR/parenting discourse: 综合素质好 = well-rounded. The father is using bureaucratic language to deliver his compliment — comic register mismatch.
应付
HSK4		yìng fuTo deal with, cope with, handle. 应付这方面的问题 = handle this kind of issue. Second sense (negative): to do something perfunctorily, just enough to get by (应付了事 = do it in a slapdash way). Context determines which sense is meant.
表态
HSK5		biǎo tàiTo state/declare one's position or stance. 明确表一下态 = clearly declare our position. 表 = express; 态 = attitude/stance. Common in political/official speech but here used by a dad in family negotiations — humorously formal register. Near-synonym: 表明立场 (more wordy).
实行
HSK4		shí xíngTo implement, put into practice, carry out (a policy/measure). 实行零限制 = implement/enforce zero restrictions. Compare 实现 (realize/achieve a goal — the outcome); 实行 focuses on putting something into practice/enforcing it (the process).
零限制
colloquial		líng xiàn zhìZero restrictions. 零 = zero; 限制 = restriction/limit. Comic exaggeration — the parents offer Xiaoxue total freedom as a manipulation tactic, so she'll feel grateful and naturally want to please them. The plan immediately backfires: she just leaves.
