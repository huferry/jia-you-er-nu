苦心
HSK5		kǔ xīnPainstaking effort, good intentions born of hardship. 苦心 implies the parents worked hard and suffered to achieve a result — here their "strategic retreat." Collocation: 一片苦心 (a whole heart of good intentions), 苦心经营 (painstakingly manage). Compare: 用心 (put in effort, more neutral).
退后
HSK4+		tuì hòuTo step back, give ground. Used here as a strategy (零限制 policy) — giving the daughter total freedom to win her trust. Literal: step backward. Figurative: make concessions. Opposite: 进逼 (press forward).
变得
grammar		biàn deTo become (+ adjective/state). 变得很坏 = become very bad. 得 here is a structural particle linking verb to result complement. Compare: 变成 (turn into + noun). Pattern: 变得 + adj is for gradual change of state; 变成 + noun is change of category.
根本不可能
HSK4		gēn běn bù kě néngAbsolutely impossible. 根本 (from the root) intensifies negation: "not possible at all." Very common spoken intensifier: 根本不知道 (have no idea), 根本不在乎 (couldn't care less). Stronger than 不太可能 (unlikely) or 没可能.
没准
colloquial		méi zhǔnMaybe, perhaps — spoken register, expressing uncertainty. 没准可以 = "Could be." Softer than 可能 and more colloquial than 也许. Often implies the speaker is not fully confident but cautiously optimistic. Very common in Beijing dialect.
可以
HSK2		kě yǐOK, fine, allowed. Here used as a comic running gag: Xiaoxue tests her parents with increasingly provocative statements and they just keep saying 可以. The repetition reveals their "zero-restriction" policy has become absurd — 可以 turns from permission into comic surrender.
