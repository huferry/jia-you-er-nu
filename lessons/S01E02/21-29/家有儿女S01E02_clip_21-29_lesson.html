HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
行使
HSK5		xíng shǐTo exercise (a right, power, or responsibility). 行使责任 = "exercise one's responsibility." Note 行 is xíng (to do/carry out) here, NOT háng (row/line). Common with legal/formal nouns: 行使权力 (exercise power), 行使职能 (perform a function).
捣蛋
colloquial		dǎo dànTo make trouble, act up, play pranks. Literally "smash an egg." 给我捣蛋 = "made trouble for me / got in my way." A casual, slightly playful term — not as serious as 闹事 (cause a scene/disturbance). Often used for mischievous children: 小捣蛋 (little troublemaker).
下马威
idiom		xià mǎ wēiTo intimidate from the outset, give someone an initial show of force. 一进门就给我们下马威 = "the moment she walked in she tried to cow us." From 马 (horse) — originally the display of authority when dismounting. Used for anyone who makes an aggressive first impression.
任人宰割
idiom		rèn rén zǎi gēTo be at someone's mercy; to allow oneself to be slaughtered. Literally "let people butcher you." An earlier scene establishes 小雪's position: she is NOT a 任人宰割的小绵羊 (meek little lamb), but a 小刺猬 (little hedgehog/porcupine full of spines). This idiom frames the whole episode's theme.
坏蛋
colloquial		huài dànRascal, bad egg, troublemaker. Affectionate-contemptuous; often used for naughty children. 两个小坏蛋 = "you two little rascals." 小坏蛋 is playful; serious: 坏人 (bad person), 坏份子 (bad element). The tone here is mock-outrage: 夏东海 is challenging the kids to a "duel."
决斗
HSK5+		jué dòuA duel, decisive confrontation. 我要跟你们决斗了 = "I'm going to have it out with you!" The word echoes the very first lesson (S01E02, 00:30) where the boys recite a love vow about dueling for 小雪. Here it circles back as 夏东海's comic threat to his own children.
