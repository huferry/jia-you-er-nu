HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
托儿
colloquial		tuō rA plant; a decoy or shill — someone secretly recruited to play a role and fool others. 托 means "to entrust/commission." 托儿 is Beijing slang; in standard Mandarin 托 alone is used. Here: Xia Xue hired a classmate to play fake boyfriend to alarm her parents.
假冒
HSK5		jiǎ màoTo impersonate, pass off as; to counterfeit. 假 (fake) + 冒 (pass off as). Common in: 假冒伪劣 (counterfeit and substandard goods). Here used humorously — impersonating a boyfriend, not a criminal act, though treated as one by the father.
叫板
colloquial		jiào bǎnTo challenge someone; to call someone's bluff; to pick a fight with. Originally from Peking opera (calling out the beat to the orchestra to start a fight scene). 拿这事给你们叫板 = use this situation to challenge/provoke you. Very colloquial, combative register.
任人宰割
idiom		rèn rén zǎi gēTo be at others' mercy; to let people do as they please with you. Literally: "let people slaughter (you) at will." A vivid image of powerlessness. The opposite of 长满刺的小刺猬 (a hedgehog covered in spines) — the contrast the boy draws to describe Xia Xue's true character.
后妈
colloquial		hòu māStepmother. More colloquial than 继母 (formal). In Chinese cultural discourse, 后妈 carries the fairy-tale stereotype of the cruel stepmother — Xia Xue fears her new stepmother will oppress her, which is the emotional root of her scheme.
失去
HSK4		shī qùTo lose (something one previously had). 失去 is permanent or serious loss; contrast with 丢 (to misplace/drop). 失去自由和快乐 = lose freedom and happiness. Collocations: 失去机会 (miss/lose an opportunity), 失去信心 (lose confidence).
