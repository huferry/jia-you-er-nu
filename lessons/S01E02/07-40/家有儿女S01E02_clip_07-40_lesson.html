HSK5		yòu zhìChildish, naive, immature. Often used by adults to dismiss young people's actions or emotions. Stronger criticism than 天真 (naive/innocent, which can be positive). 幼稚园 = kindergarten. Here paired with 不负责任 as a double dismissal.
