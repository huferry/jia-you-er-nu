WordPinyinExplanation
落到…手掌里
idiom		luò dào…shǒu zhǎng lǐTo fall into someone's clutches/hands. 手掌 = palm. The image is of a child falling back into the grip of the parents after the police leave. The logic: calling 110 is pointless since the police leave and you're still at home. A dark but funny observation on the limits of child protection.
堵上
HSK4+		dǔ shàngTo block up, plug up, stop up. 把嘴给堵上了 = "mouth has been completely blocked up" — i.e., left with nothing to say. The 把-construction + 给 (adds emphasis/adversarial nuance). 堵 also used for traffic: 堵车 (traffic jam). Here: an argument so good it silences you.
彼此
HSK5		bǐ cǐEach other; mutually (between both parties). 彼此沟通 = communicate with each other. More formal/literary than 互相. Used in formal speech or writing. Can stand alone as "you and me both" in colloquial responses: 彼此彼此 = "same to you!" (mutually).
沟通
HSK4		gōu tōngTo communicate; communication. 想沟通一下 = want to have a communication/chat. 沟通 implies a productive exchange — not just talking (说话) but achieving mutual understanding. Very common in modern Chinese: 沟通能力 (communication skills), 沟通不畅 (poor communication).
就算了
colloquial		jiù suàn leLet's just forget about it; let that go; that settles it. 为了狂野男孩的事就算了 = regarding the Wild Boy business, let's just drop it. 算了 alone = forget it / never mind. 就 adds finality: "then we'll count it as resolved." Very common for dismissing sensitive topics.
讨论
HSK3		tǎo lùnTo discuss; discussion. 不想讨论我的男朋友 = don't want to discuss my boyfriend. More neutral than 争论 (argue/dispute) or 辩论 (debate, formal). 讨论 implies two-way exchange of views. Collocation: 讨论会 (discussion forum), 课堂讨论 (classroom discussion).
