玫瑰花
HSK4		méi guī huāRose. The book Wild Boy is reading aloud uses classic romantic lines from a novel — "送给你一朵玫瑰花" is a clichéd love declaration, which is exactly why it's funny here. 玫瑰 alone = rose (the plant/flower concept); 玫瑰花 = a rose (the actual flower). Collocations: 一束玫瑰花 (a bouquet of roses), 玫瑰色 (rose-colored).
目标
HSK4		mù biāoGoal, objective, target. 达到目标 = achieve one's goal. Here Xiaoxue means her plan — using Wild Boy as a decoy — hasn't fully worked yet. Collocations: 完成目标 (complete a goal), 实现目标 (realize a goal). Note: 目标 is for a concrete intended outcome; compare 理想 (ideal/dream, more abstract).
乖乖女
colloquial		guāi guāi nǚGood girl, obedient daughter. 乖 = well-behaved, docile. Reduplication 乖乖 adds affectionate/dismissive tone. 乖乖女 = a girl who follows rules and pleases parents. Used here by the father observing a change in Xiaoxue's behavior — slightly patronizing but warm. Opposite: 叛逆女 (rebellious girl).
宽容

Collocations: 宽容大度 (magnanimous), 对…宽容 (be tolerant toward). Compare: 包容 (encompass/forgive, slightly broader); 原谅 (forgive a specific wrong).
英明
HSK5		yīng míngWise, brilliant (of a decision or leader). Formal/literary register — often used to praise emperors or leaders. Here used self-ironically by the father to congratulate himself, adding comic pomposity. Collocations: 英明决策 (brilliant decision), 英明领导 (wise leadership). The humor: praising your own parenting in imperial vocabulary.
