WordPinyinExplanation
行李
HSK4		xíng liLuggage, baggage. 拿行李 = carry/take the luggage; 行李箱 = suitcase. Note the neutral tone on 李 in natural speech. Common at airports/train stations: 托运行李 (checked baggage), 随身行李 (carry-on).
咱俩
colloquial		zán liǎThe two of us (inclusive). 咱 is informal Northern/Beijing dialect for "we/us (inclusive, including the listener)". 俩 = liǎ in colloquial speech (= 两个). Compare: 我们俩 (more standard); 咱们 (all of us, inclusive). Very common in Beijing-dialect TV.
后妈
HSK4+		hòu māStepmother. 后 = behind/after, here meaning "replacement/subsequent". Compare: 继母 jì mǔ (formal/literary term for stepmother); 后爸 (stepfather). 娶 qǔ = to marry (used when a man marries — 娶妻, 娶了后妈).
娶
HSK4		qǔTo marry (a woman) — used for the groom's action. Opposite: 嫁 jià = to marry (used for the bride's action). Pattern: 男人娶女人，女人嫁男人. 娶了后妈以后 = after (dad) married a stepmother. Never use 娶 for the bride's perspective.
过得好
HSK3+		guò de hǎoTo be getting on well, to live well. 过 (verb) = to live/get along; 得 (complement marker); 好 = well. Pattern: 过得好/不好/怎么样. Compare: 过得怎么样？(How are you getting on?); 日子过得不错 (life is going pretty well). 你过得好吗 is a warmer, more concerned "are you doing alright" than a casual 你好吗.
咱们
HSK3		zán menWe/us — inclusive (includes the listener). Contrasts with 我们 which can be exclusive (may exclude the listener). 咱们姓夏的孩子 = we kids with the surname Xia (Xiaoxue addresses her brother, including both of them). Common in Northern Mandarin/Beijing speech; less common in Southern dialects.
