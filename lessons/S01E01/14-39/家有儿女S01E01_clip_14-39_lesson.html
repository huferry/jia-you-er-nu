WordPinyinExplanation
偏心
HSK4+		piān xīnFavoritism, partiality, to favor one person over another. 太偏心了 = that's such favoritism! Here the younger boy complains that the soup was made especially for Xiaoxue (too many eggs = made with extra care). 偏 = biased, tilted; 心 = heart. Collocations: 偏心眼儿 (colloquial: playing favorites); 一碗水端平 (hold the bowl level = treat everyone equally — the ideal).
委屈
HSK4		wěi quTo feel wronged, aggrieved; a sense of grievance/injustice. Both noun and verb. 好委屈 = feeling very wronged. 说出委屈 = voice one's grievances. Key nuance: 委屈 implies the person has been treated unfairly and is suppressing hurt feelings — not just sadness but specifically the feeling of injustice. Very commonly used in family/relationship contexts.
趁着
HSK4+		chèn zheTaking advantage of (an opportunity/condition); while (something is the case). 趁着 + situation + action. 有什么委屈你趁着都说出来 = while you have the chance, get all your grievances out. 趁热打铁 = strike while the iron is hot (idiom). 趁 alone is used the same way: 趁机 = take the opportunity; 趁早 = the sooner the better.
道歉
HSK4		dào qiànTo apologize. 我替她向你道歉 = I'll apologize to you on her behalf. Pattern: 向 + person + 道歉. 替 = on behalf of. Compare: 对不起 (sorry — informal, immediate); 道歉 is more formal and deliberate. 请求原谅 (to ask for forgiveness — even more formal). 刘美 offers this as a way to defuse Xiaoxue's emotional state — a pragmatic rather than sincere apology.
好像
HSK3		hǎo xiàngIt seems like, as if, apparently. 咱们好像有好长时间没住在一起了 = it seems like we haven't lived together for such a long time. Used to express uncertainty or approximation. Compare: 似乎 (sìhū — more literary); 看起来 (looks like — based on visual observation); 好像 is the most colloquial and versatile.
一看就
grammar		yī kàn jiùAs soon as one looks, it's immediately clear. Pattern: 一 + V + 就 + result. 我一看就知道 = I knew the moment I looked. 一 + verb signals the trigger; 就 signals the immediate consequence. This pattern expresses that something is immediately obvious. Here: 我一看就是好委屈 = one look and I can tell she's feeling very aggrieved.
