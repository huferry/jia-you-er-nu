WordPinyinExplanation
等…以后才…
grammar		děng … yǐhòu cái …'Not until … can …': 得等姐姐来了以后才能吃 = we can only eat once she arrives. 才 marks 'only then (and later than you'd like)'. 得 = děi (must).
肚子
HSK4		dùzi'Belly, tummy' (dù, 4th tone). 肚子叫 = stomach rumbling; 肚子疼 = stomachache. Careful: dǔzi (3rd tone) means tripe (food)! The line parodies the pop song 《你快回来》.
出事
HSK5		chūshì'Something (bad) happens, have an accident'. 会不会出什么事 = could something have happened? Neutral alternatives: 发生 (happen), 出问题 (go wrong).
下馆子
idiom		xià guǎnzi'Eat out at a restaurant' (colloquial). 馆子 = eatery. Neutral equivalent: 去饭店吃饭 / 在外面吃.
要不
HSK5		yàobù'How about… / Otherwise': 要不咱们先吃吧 = how about we eat first? Suggests an alternative plan; same as 要不然 / 不如.
凉了都
grammar		liáng le dōu= 都凉了 'it's all gone cold'. Moving 都 to the end is an afterthought word order typical of casual Beijing speech.
半道上
colloq.		bàndào shang'Halfway, on the way' = 半路上. 半道上饿得受不了 = got starving partway there.
受不了
HSK5		shòu bu liǎo'Can't stand / can't bear it' — potential complement. 了 is liǎo here, not le. Adj + 得 + 受不了 = so … you can't stand it: 饿得受不了 (starving), 热得受不了.
像…似的
grammar		xiàng … shìde'Just like …': 就像我们俩似的 = just like us two. 似的 is pronounced shìde (not sìde) in this pattern; the same as 像…一样.
