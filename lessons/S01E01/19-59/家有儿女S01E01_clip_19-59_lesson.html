HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
第…页
HSK3		dì … yèPage number. 第四十八页 = page 48. 第 marks ordinal numbers; 页 = page. Dad assigns 刘星 lines 3-16 of page 48 as punishment — the same love poem Xiaoxue's boyfriend 狂野男孩 recites later (and again at the S01E02 opening).
摘
HSK4		zhāiTo remove, take off (hat, glasses, fruit). 把帽子摘下来 = take your hat off. 摘 implies detaching something that was attached or worn. Compare: 脱 (take off clothing — 脱衣服, 脱鞋); 摘 is for smaller accessories and picking fruit. 摘眼镜 = take off glasses.
染
HSK5		rǎnTo dye; to tint; to infect. 染成黑色 = dye it black. 染 + 成 + color = dye into a color. 染发 = dye one's hair. The scene: 刘星 has dyed or bleached his hair, which won't wash out before school — Dad panics.
干嘛
colloquial		gàn máWhat are you doing? / Why are you doing that? Colloquial form of 干什么. 你干嘛呀 = what do you think you're doing?! The repeated 我干嘛呀 我干嘛呀 is 刘星 mimicking/mocking Dad's question back at him — a classic kid deflection tactic.
非…不可
HSK5		fēi … bù kěMust/have to (emphatic). 非去不可 = absolutely must go / have no choice but to go. Double negative construction: 非 (not) + V + 不可 (not allowed) = insisting on the action. Stronger than 一定要. Compare: 非做不可 (must do it no matter what).
洗得掉 / 洗不掉
grammar		xǐ de diào / xǐ bù diàoPotential complement: can/cannot wash off. 得 (de) = potential marker (can); 不 = cannot. Pattern: V + 得/不 + result complement. 洗掉 = wash off (result); 洗得掉 = can be washed off; 洗不掉 = cannot be washed off. Same pattern: 拿得动/拿不动 (can/cannot carry).
