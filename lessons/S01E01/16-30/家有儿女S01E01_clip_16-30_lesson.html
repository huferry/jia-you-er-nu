Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
谁都不
grammar		shéi dōu bùNot … anyone; not … at all. 谁都不叫我 = doesn't call me anything (lit. "doesn't call me, whoever"). Pattern: 谁都不 + V = nobody (or not … anyone). Compare 什么都不 (nothing at all), 哪儿都不 (nowhere at all). The 都 here is a universal quantifier: it intensifies the negation.
连…都/也…
HSK4		lián … dōu/yěEven … (not). 连个头都不回 = she doesn't even turn her head. Pattern: 连 + NP + 都/也 + (neg.) V. Used to highlight the most obvious or minimal thing that is (or isn't) done. Very common in spoken Chinese to express frustration or emphasis.
上哪儿
colloquial		shàng nǎrWhere to? / Where did (you/she) go? Colloquial Beijing contraction of 上哪儿去. 上 here = going to (direction verb). Common spoken pattern: 上哪儿去了 = where did you go? / where'd she go? Also heard as 去哪儿了 in more standard speech. The repeated questions (上哪儿去了 / 上哪儿了) show the two grandmothers echoing each other in confusion.
箱子
HSK4		xiāng ziSuitcase, box, trunk. 没看见箱子还在 = don't you see the suitcase is still here? A key plot clue: Xiaoxue's suitcase being left behind means she hasn't run away back to grandpa's. 还 here = still (hái). 箱 alone = chest/box; common compounds: 行李箱 (luggage), 冰箱 (refrigerator, lit. "ice box").
说明
HSK4		shuō míngTo indicate, to show, to prove; an explanation. 这说明啊 = that means / that shows. As a verb: 说明 + clause (this shows that…). As a noun: 使用说明 (user manual / instructions). Often followed by a logical conclusion — here 夏东海 is building suspense before his deduction about Xiaoxue.
更
HSK3		gèngEven more, still more. 我更不知道 = I know even less (lit. "I even more don't know"). Degree adverb used to escalate a comparison. Compare: 还 (hái) = still; 更 = even more so. Pattern: A + neg. V, B + 更 + neg. V = if A doesn't, B does so even less.
