WordPinyinExplanation
信任
HSK4		xìnrèn'To trust' (someone's character/reliability). 我对你们…不信任 = 'I have no trust in you'. Compare 相信 (to believe — what someone says).
一点都不
grammar		yìdiǎn dōu bù'Not … at all': 一点(儿) + 都/也 + 不/没 + verb/adj. 我一点都不信任 = I don't trust (you) one bit. Same pattern as 一点…都没有.
荔枝
noun		lìzhī'Lychee' — a southern Chinese fruit, a bit of a treat in Beijing. 荔枝呀! = 'Ooh, lychees!' (the kids spotting them).
一边站着
colloq.		yìbiān zhànzhe'Stand aside / out of the way'. 一边 = to one side; 着 marks staying in that position. Often with 去: 一边去! = 'Go away!'
跟…有什么关系
grammar		gēn … yǒu shénme guānxi'What does it have to do with …?' — a rhetorical brush-off meaning 'none of your business'. 关系 (HSK3) = connection, relationship.
缺
HSK4		quē'To lack, be short of': 还缺点什么 = 'still missing something'. 缺钱 (short of money), 缺人 (short-staffed). Related: 缺点 (shortcoming) — not the same thing as 缺 + 点 here!
盒
HSK3		héMeasure word for boxes: 一盒奶糖 = a box of milk candy, 一盒饼干. Also a noun: 盒子 (box).
…了吧你
grammar		…le ba nǐTeasing tag: statement + 吧 + 你 at the end = 'You do, don't you!' Repeating the subject at the end (你想吃奶糖了吧你) is very colloquial Beijing speech.
编排
HSK6		biānpái'To arrange, put together (a performance)': 编排节目 = choreograph/write a show. (Also, colloquially, 'to make up stories about someone'.)
节目
HSK3		jiémù'Program, show, act' — TV programs, or a performance at a party. 小节目 = a little skit. Measure word 个.
演演
grammar		yǎnyanReduplicated verb = 'do it a bit, have a go': 给你演演 = 'perform it for you'. Second syllable goes toneless, like 看看 (kànkan).
