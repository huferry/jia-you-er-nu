WordPinyinExplanation
耐心
HSK3		nài xīnPatience (noun); patient (adjective). 爱心耐心 = love and patience — 夏东海's mantra for blending the family. 耐心 as adj.: 要耐心 (you need to be patient). As noun: 有耐心 (to have patience), 失去耐心 (lose patience). The repetition of 耐心 at 19:13 echoes this earlier scene and ties the episode together.
宽大为怀
HSK6/idiom		kuān dà wéi huáiTo be magnanimous, to treat others with generosity and forgiveness. Chengyu: 宽大 = broad/lenient, 为 = to be (wéi), 怀 = heart/mind. Used here as 夏东海's parenting philosophy — be big-hearted toward Xiaoxue. Note 为 is wéi (linking verb "to be"), not wèi (for the sake of).
收拾
HSK4		shōu shiTo tidy up, to put in order; also colloquially: to deal with someone / to fix someone. 我给她收拾收拾 = I'll tidy up (her room) for her. The reduplication 收拾收拾 softens and casualizes the action. Watch the double meaning: in other contexts 收拾你 = I'll sort you out (a threat). Here it's gentle.
福分
HSK5+		fú fenOne's lot of blessings; good fortune (as predestined). 娶了你是我最大的福分 = marrying you is my greatest blessing. 分 (fèn) here = one's allotted portion/fate, NOT fēn (minute). Compare 福气 (good fortune, luck) — similar but 福分 has a stronger sense of destiny/fate. A heartfelt, slightly literary expression.
肯定
HSK4		kěn dìngDefinitely, certainly (adverb); to affirm (verb); affirmative (adj.). 咱们肯定能做得更好 = we can definitely do even better. As a verb: 我肯定这件事 = I confirm this matter. Very common spoken intensifier. Compare 一定 (yīdìng) — also "definitely/must", but 肯定 is more conversational and confident.
做得
grammar		zuò deDegree complement with 得. 做得很好 = did it very well; 做得更好 = do it even better. Pattern: V + 得 + degree. 得 here is a complement marker (not 的 or 地). Contrast: 做好 (accomplish it / do it well — result), vs 做得好 (do it well — manner/degree). Very productive pattern: 说得清楚, 跑得快, 睡得香.
