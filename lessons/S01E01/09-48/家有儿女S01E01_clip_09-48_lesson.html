WordPinyinExplanation
咱们
HSK3		zánmenWe/us (inclusive) — includes the listener, unlike 我们 which can exclude them. 咱们生活在一块 = we (all of us together) will live in one place. In Beijing colloquial speech 咱 alone can mean I/me or we.
欺负
HSK4		qīfuTo bully, pick on, take advantage of. 不许欺负人家 = not allowed to bully her. 人家 here refers to a third person (Xiaoxue). Common collocations: 欺负弱小, 被人欺负.
不许
HSK4		bù xǔNot allowed to, must not. Stronger and more authoritative than 不要 or 别. Pattern: 不许 + verb. Typical parent-to-child command. Cf. 禁止 (forbidden, formal/written).
奖
HSK4		jiǎngTo reward; a reward/prize. 奖你一片火腿肠 = reward you with a slice of sausage. Colloquial: 奖励 is the more formal word. The joke here is the absurdly humble reward (cheap sausage) for such earnest behavior.
火腿肠
colloquial		huǒtuǐ chángProcessed ham sausage — the cheap, ubiquitous snack stick sold at every corner shop. In Chinese family comedies, offering 火腿肠 as a "prize" is a running joke about the gap between a child's eagerness and the parent's stinginess/humor.
别…了
grammar		bié … leStop/don't (do something). 别去了 = don't bother going / stop (about to go). The 了 signals a change of plan or that the action is no longer needed. Contrast: 别去 (simple prohibition) vs 别去了 (canceling an action already in progress or about to start).
