WordPinyinExplanation
闻
HSK5		wén'To smell' (verb): 闻一闻 = 'take a sniff'. Not to be confused with 嗅 (literary) or 香/臭 (adjectives for the smell itself). 蚊子一闻 = 'the moment the mosquito smells it' — 一…就… pattern implied.
换
HSK3		huàn'To change / swap'. 换衣服 = change clothes; also 换钱, 换工作, 换个方式. Different from 变 (to become) — 换 replaces one thing with another.
胡说
HSK5		húshuō'To talk nonsense'. 别胡说 is a stock scolding phrase; 胡 (recklessly) also forms 胡闹, 胡来.
时差
HSK6		shíchā'Time difference / jet lag'. 倒时差 (dǎo shíchā) is the spoken phrase for adjusting to jet lag; 倒 here means 'to shift/switch', hence 倒过来 'switch over'. Reading dǎo, not dào.
瞎说八道
idiom		xiā shuō bā dào'To talk utter nonsense'. 瞎 (blind, aimlessly) + 说 + 八道 (filler intensifier). More emphatic than 胡说; usually said in irritation.
环游
HSK6		huányóu'To travel around (the world)'. 环游世界 / 环游地球. Formal-ish verb; the joke here alludes to a famous 'around the world in 80 days' story.
地球
HSK4		dìqiú'The Earth, the globe'. 地球村 = global village; 保护地球 = protect the Earth. Compare 世界 (world, in the human/political sense).
一百八十天了
grammar		yì bǎi bā shí tiān le都…了 pattern: 你从美国都回来一百八十天了 = 'you've already been back for 180 days'. 都 + duration + 了 stresses that a lot of time has passed, hinting at impatience.

grammar		hái méi …'Still hasn't yet': 你还没倒过时差来. 还没 + verb + (complement) signals an expected event hasn't happened; 过来 marks 'switching back to normal'.
