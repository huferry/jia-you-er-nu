HSK5		zhēnxīn'Sincere, wholehearted': 真心的欢迎 = 'sincerely welcome'. Stronger than 真的 (really) — 真心 stresses that the feeling comes from the heart, not just words.
完整
HSK4		wánzhěng'Complete, whole' — nothing missing. 才能算完整 = 'can only then be called complete'. Compare 完全 (completely, an adverb) vs 完整 (an adjective describing a whole thing).

grammar		cái néng'Only then can...': …了，X才能… — the first clause states a condition, 才 marks that only once it's met does X become possible. 她来了 咱们这个新家才能算完整.
