HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
尊重
HSK4		zūn zhòngTo respect; respect (noun). 受尊重 = to be respected (受 = to receive/suffer). Collocations: 互相尊重 (mutual respect), 尊重别人 (respect others), 受到尊重 (receive respect). Contrast: 看不起 (look down on), 歧视 (discriminate against). Key word for understanding Chinese family/social dynamics.
人身自由
formal/legal		rén shēn zì yóuPersonal freedom, freedom of the person. 人身 = one's person/body; 自由 = freedom. A formal legal/political term that Xiaoxue applies comically to household rules. 有人身自由吗 = do you have personal liberty? Note: 自由 alone = freedom (broader); 人身自由 specifically refers to freedom of movement/personal autonomy.
反正
HSK4		fǎn zhèngAnyway, in any case, regardless. Used to introduce a conclusion or state something that holds true no matter what. 反正…就… = no matter what…, (the result is)…. Compare: 总之 zǒng zhī (in a word, in short — more formal); 不管怎样 (no matter how). Very common in spoken Chinese. Here: Xiaoyu's deadpan answer reveals the household rule: no feet-washing = no bed.
绝对
HSK4		jué duìAbsolutely, definitely; absolute. Used as an intensifier. 绝对不能 = absolutely cannot. Doubled for emphasis: 绝对绝对不能 = absolutely 100% cannot. Compare: 肯定 kěn dìng (definitely/certainly — more about speaker's confidence); 一定 yī dìng (must/certainly — often about obligation or prediction). 绝对 often carries a more emphatic, final tone.
欺负
HSK4		qī fuTo bully, to pick on, to take advantage of. 欺负过你吗 = has (he) ever bullied you? (过 = experiential aspect). 受欺负/被欺负 = to be bullied. Near-synonym: 欺压 qī yā (oppress, stronger); 欺骗 qī piàn (deceive). Common in colloquial speech about children and workplace dynamics.
定位
HSK5		dìng wèiPositioning, role, place (in a system). Originally from navigation (determine position). Now widely used: 我在这个家的定位 = my positioning/role in this family. 品牌定位 (brand positioning), 职业定位 (career positioning). Xiaoxue uses it sarcastically: her entire "role" in the family is apparently just not to be bullied — a wryly low definition.
生存起点
colloquial		shēng cún qǐ diǎnStarting point for survival; baseline for existence. 生存 = survival; 起点 = starting point. 也太低了吧 = it's just too low, isn't it (也 intensifies; 吧 seeks agreement or expresses mild exasperation). The rhetorical structure 也太…了吧 is a very useful spoken pattern for expressing "that's a bit much / that's going too far."
