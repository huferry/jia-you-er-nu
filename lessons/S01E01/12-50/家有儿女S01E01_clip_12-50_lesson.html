WordPinyinExplanation
一会儿
HSK3		yī huì rA moment, a little while. 一会儿就熟 = will be cooked in a moment (就 = right away/then). 再等一会儿 = wait a little longer. Can refer to past or future short time: 一会儿我来 (I'll come in a bit); 刚才一会儿 (just a moment ago). Note the erhua (儿化) — typical Beijing speech. Compare: 一下 (a moment — often for quick actions: 等一下 = wait a sec).
污染
HSK5		wū rǎnPollution, contamination; to pollute/contaminate. 被污染过 = has been contaminated (被 = passive marker; 过 = experiential aspect). Environmental concern: 水污染 (water pollution), 空气污染 (air pollution), 污染源 (source of pollution). Xiaoxue applies food-safety anxiety to the crab — a comic character moment. 污染 is now one of the most common news/policy words in modern Chinese.
防伪标志
HSK5+		fáng wèi biāo zhìAnti-counterfeit label/mark. 防 = prevent; 伪 = fake/counterfeit; 标志 = mark/label/sign. In China, premium products (especially 大闸蟹, liquor, medicine) carry holographic or QR anti-fake labels. Xiaoxue asks if the crab has one — played for laughs. 标志 alone = mark, logo, symbol (国家标志 = national emblem). Compare: 防伪 (anti-counterfeiting, as a verb/adj compound).
正宗
HSK4+		zhèng zōngAuthentic, genuine, orthodox. 正宗的大闸蟹 = authentic hairy crabs (from Yangcheng Lake). Collocations: 正宗北京烤鸭 (authentic Peking duck), 正宗四川火锅 (genuine Sichuan hotpot). Compare: 地道 dì dao (genuine, authentic — more colloquial, often about accent or food); 真正 zhēn zhèng (truly, genuinely — more about essence/nature). 正宗 is specifically about origin and tradition.
大闸蟹
cultural		dà zhá xièShanghai hairy crab; Chinese mitten crab. 大 = big; 闸 = sluice/gate (refers to the lake waterways); 蟹 = crab. The most prized variety comes from Yangcheng Lake (阳澄湖) in Jiangsu. Seasonally eaten in autumn; authentic ones have hologram labels (防伪标志) around their legs. A luxury food item and a byword for food authenticity debates in China.
主见
HSK5		zhǔ jiànOne's own opinion/view; independent judgment. 没有主见 = has no mind of one's own, easily swayed. 您已经没有主见了 = you've already lost your own opinion (sarcastic: you've been brainwashed into agreeing). Compare: 见解 jiàn jiě (view, perspective — more neutral/intellectual); 意见 yì jiàn (opinion — broader). 有主见 is a compliment (decisive, opinionated); 没主见 is a criticism (spineless, easily led).
