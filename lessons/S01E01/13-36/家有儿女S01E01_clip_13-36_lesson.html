座位
HSK3		zuò wèiSeat, posture (here used colloquially for 坐姿 sitting posture). Standard meaning: a seat (on a bus, in a theater). Dad is using it humorously/loosely to mean 坐姿 (sitting posture/bearing). 比赛 = competition. 特等奖 = top/special prize (特等 = special grade, above 一等奖).
独立人格
HSK5+		dú lì rén géIndependent personhood / autonomous personality. 独立 = independent (HSK4); 人格 = character, personhood, dignity. A formal/legal-sounding phrase — Xiaoxue is reading out a quasi-legal declaration of rights, which is the comedy here. Collocations: 尊重人格 (respect personhood), 人格尊严 (personal dignity).
衣食住行
idiom		yī shí zhù xíngFood, clothing, shelter, and transportation — the four necessities of life. A fixed four-character idiom (成语). Covers all basic aspects of daily living. Commonly used in political speeches and everyday contexts: 老百姓的衣食住行 = the basic needs of ordinary people.
保持
HSK4		bǎo chíTo maintain, keep, preserve. 保持个性 = maintain one's individuality. 保持沉默 = maintain silence (appears later in the episode). 保持联系 = keep in touch. Verb + object structure: 保持 + noun/state.
不容质疑
idiom		bù róng zhì yíBeyond question, indisputable, brooking no doubt. 不容 = not tolerating/allowing; 质疑 = to question, to doubt. A formal, emphatic phrase. Used in written language and formal speeches. Xiaoxue using it at the dinner table to announce her household rules is deliberately over-the-top and comedic.
随便
HSK3		suí biànCasually, randomly, arbitrarily; to do as one pleases. 不许随便动 = not allowed to casually touch. As an adjective: 随便 = casual, informal (你随便 = whatever you like / up to you). As an adverb modifying verbs: 不要随便说话 = don't just say anything without thinking.
