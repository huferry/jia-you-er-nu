被
HSK3		bèiPassive marker: 我的手被虫子咬了 = 'my hand was bitten by a bug'. Pattern: Receiver + 被 + (Agent) + Verb + result. Here 给 is added before the verb for colloquial emphasis (被…给咬了).
蚊子
HSK6		wénzi'Mosquito'. Common collocations: 蚊子叮 / 被蚊子咬. Note 叮 (dīng) is the usual verb in writing; 咬 is the everyday spoken choice.
挠
beyond HSK		náo'To scratch (an itch) lightly'. 挠痒痒 = scratch an itch. Not the same as 抓 (grab/claw) — 挠 is gentle and soothing, which is why a parent says 给挠挠.
可怜
HSK5		kělián'Pitiful, poor thing'. As a sympathetic exclamation, 可怜了 / 真可怜 comforts a child. Can also mean 'to pity' as a verb: 可怜他.
胡说
HSK5		húshuō'To talk nonsense'. 别胡说 is a stock scolding phrase; 胡 (recklessly) + verb forms 胡说八道, 胡闹, 胡来.
不都…吗
grammar		bù dōu … maRhetorical question: 爸爸妈妈不都在呢吗 = 'aren't Mom and Dad both here?' Using 不 + 都 + 吗 expects a 'yes' and reassures. 呢 adds a gentle, explanatory tone.
甜
HSK4		tián'Sweet'. Used for taste (甜的血), also figuratively: 嘴甜 'sweet-talking', 笑得很甜 'a sweet smile'. Opposite: 苦.
臭
HSK5		chòu'Stinky, smelly'. Opposite of 香 (fragrant). Also figurative: 臭脾气 'bad temper'. Here it's the playful counterpart to 甜.
血
HSK5		xuè'Blood'. Formal reading xuè; spoken Mandarin (esp. Beijing) often says xiě, e.g. 流血 liú xiě. Both accepted.
只…不…
grammar		zhǐ … bù …'Only (does A), not (B)': 蚊子只咬我不咬刘星. 只 limits the action to one target, then 不 contrasts with the excluded one.
