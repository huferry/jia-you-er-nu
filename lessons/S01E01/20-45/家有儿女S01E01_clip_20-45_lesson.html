WordPinyinExplanation
耐心
HSK4		nài xīnPatient; patience. 耐 = endure; 心 = heart/mind. 对小雪真有耐心 = truly patient with Xiaoxue. Used as adjective (耐心的人 = a patient person) or noun (有耐心 = have patience). The word appears three times in this episode — a running theme for dealing with Xiaoxue.
佩服
HSK4		pèi fúTo admire, have deep respect for. 我真佩服 = I truly admire you. Stronger than 尊重 (respect). Often used sincerely or ironically. 佩服佩服 (repeated) = genuine admiration or mock admiration depending on tone. Here it is 刘美's sister complimenting her calm demeanor.
宽大为怀
chengyu		kuān dà wéi huáiTo be magnanimous / generous in spirit. Four-character idiom: 宽大 = broad/generous; 为怀 = to hold in one's heart/mind. Used to praise someone's large-hearted tolerance. 刘美 lists 爱心·耐心·宽大为怀 as her personal virtues when dealing with her new stepdaughter.
急
HSK3		jíAnxious, in a hurry, urgent. 你急我都不急 = you may be anxious — I'm not at all. 急 can be a stative verb (be anxious) or adjective (urgent). Common expressions: 着急 (feel anxious/worried); 急忙 (hurriedly); 急事 (urgent matter). Contrast: 急 (internal anxiety) vs 忙 (busy with tasks).
介绍
HSK3		jiè shàoTo introduce; introduction. 介绍一下 = let me make an introduction / allow me to introduce. The 一下 softens it into a casual, brief action. 自我介绍 = self-introduction; 介绍信 = letter of introduction. Xiaoxue uses this phrase before revealing her surprise boyfriend.
