HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
开饭
HSK3+		kāi fànDinner/meal is ready; to start eating. 开饭了 = it's time to eat! (literally "the meal opens"). Common household announcement. Compare: 吃饭了 (let's eat / time to eat — can be used similarly); 开饭 is slightly more formal/announcement-like. 饭 here = meal (not specifically rice).
旁边
HSK3		páng biānBeside, next to, by the side of. 在你旁边 = beside you / next to you. Positional noun — used after 在: 在桌子旁边 (by the table). Compare: 旁边 (beside), 附近 (nearby/in the vicinity), 身边 (at one's side — more intimate). 我就在你旁边 = I'll be right next to you (就 adds reassurance).
大虾
everyday		dà xiāLarge shrimp, prawns. 虾 = shrimp/prawn in general; 大虾 = big ones (a treat). Note: 大虾 is also a humorous internet slang term for "expert/guru" (大侠 homophone joke), but here it's literal food. The father is trying to make Xiaoxue feel welcome with a nice spread.
健康观念
HSK4+		jiàn kāng guān niànHealth concept/philosophy; view on health. 健康 = health/healthy; 观念 = concept, idea, mindset. 正确的健康观念 = the correct health philosophy. Xiaoxue deflects the stepmother's hospitality with a "health" lecture — a character moment showing her prickliness. 观念 collocates: 传统观念 (traditional values), 更新观念 (update one's thinking).
少吃点
grammar pattern		shǎo chī diǎnEat a little less. Pattern: 少/多 + V + 点 = V a little less/more. 多吃点 (eat more) vs 少吃点 (eat less). 点 softens the imperative. This pattern is ubiquitous: 多喝水 (drink more water), 少说废话 (talk less nonsense), 多注意 (pay more attention). Xiaoxue counters 多吃点 with this correction.
第一天
HSK3		dì yī tiānThe first day. 你这不是今天第一天来吗 = isn't today your first day (here)? The "不是…吗" structure is a rhetorical question implying "it obviously is": 不是…吗 = isn't it the case that…? Used to gently challenge or express mild surprise. Compare: 是不是…? (a yes/no question); 不是…吗 carries more rhetorical weight.
