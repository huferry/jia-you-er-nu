WordPinyinExplanation
枕巾
HSK5+		zhěn jīnPillow cover / pillow towel. 枕 = pillow; 巾 = cloth/towel. 枕巾 is a rectangular towel placed over a pillowcase — common in mainland Chinese homes. Xiaoxue objects to the ones prepared for her and threatens to repurpose them.
擦脚布
colloquial		cā jiǎo bùFoot wipe / foot towel. 擦 = to wipe/rub; 脚 = foot; 布 = cloth. Not a standard lexicon item — it is a colloquial compound. The humor: Xiaoxue threatens to demote her 枕巾 to 擦脚布 status; the boys ask if they can do the reverse.
把…当…
grammar		bǎ … dāng …To treat/use X as Y. 把擦脚布当枕巾 = use a foot wipe as a pillow cover. Pattern: 把 + object + 当(成) + role. 当 (dāng) here means "to serve as / to treat as". Distinguish from 当 (dàng) = to regard wrongly (误当). This pattern appears three times in the scene.
脚丫子
colloquial		jiǎo yā ziFeet (informal/affectionate). 脚 = foot; 丫子 is a colloquial suffix that makes it playful/childlike. 两个脚丫子 = both feet. Used by the boys to comic effect as they lobby for equal treatment.
捣乱
HSK4+		dǎo luànTo make trouble, stir things up, cause mischief. 捣 = to pound/stir; 乱 = chaos/disorder. 老在这捣什么乱呢 = what are you always making trouble here for? Often used by adults scolding children. Compare 闹事 (cause a disturbance — more serious).
不能换
HSK3		bù néng huànCannot swap / no swapping allowed. 换 = to exchange/swap. Dad's ruling: objects have their proper function and cannot be substituted. 换 is versatile: 换衣服 (change clothes), 换钱 (exchange money), 交换 (exchange mutually).
