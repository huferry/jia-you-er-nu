HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
专门
HSK4		zhuān ménSpecially, specifically, exclusively. 专门做的 = made especially (for you). Compare: 特意 tè yì (deliberately, on purpose — implies effort made); 特别 tè bié (especially — more adjectival). 专门 often modifies the purpose: 专门为你 (specifically for you), 专门学习中文 (study Chinese exclusively/dedicatedly). Very common collocate: 专门来/去 (come/go specifically to).
习惯
HSK3		xí guànTo be used to / accustomed to (verb); a habit (noun). 习惯 + V = be used to doing. 不习惯 = not used to. 习惯别人 = get used to other people. This scene is a wordplay on the word: 多吃点 → rebuttal → 大家互相慢慢习惯 → 那恐怕得你们习惯我了 → 反正我是不会习惯别人的. The repetition builds Xiaoxue's character. Compare: 适应 shì yìng (to adapt — more effortful, deliberate change).
恐怕
HSK4		kǒng pàI'm afraid, probably, I suspect (hedging adverb). 恐怕得你们习惯我了 = I'm afraid you'll have to get used to me. Signals that something is undesirable or unexpected but likely. Compare: 也许 yě xǔ (maybe — neutral); 大概 dà gài (probably — more neutral estimation); 恐怕 implies the speaker foresees something the listener may not want to hear.
反正
HSK4		fǎn zhèngAnyway, regardless, in any case. 反正我是不会习惯别人的 = I'm not going to adapt to anyone else, period. 反正 dismisses alternatives and states a fixed conclusion. Useful spoken pattern: 反正…就… (whatever the case, the outcome is…). Xiaoxue uses it twice in the episode (also at 11:11), marking her as stubborn and self-certain. Very colloquial; too casual for formal writing.
横着走
colloquial		héng zhe zǒuTo walk sideways. 横 = horizontal/sideways; 着 (zhe) = manner complement marker (ongoing state). 横着走 = walking in a horizontal/sideways manner — Xiaoyu's charming indirect description of a crab without knowing the word. 着 (zhe) attaches to verbs to indicate manner or ongoing state: 躺着看书 (read lying down), 站着等 (wait standing up). Distinguish from 着 zhāo (move in chess/strategy).
八条腿
everyday		bā tiáo tuǐEight legs. 条 is the measure word for long, strip-like things — legs, rivers, roads, fish, trousers. 八条腿横着走那个 = that thing with eight legs that walks sideways. Xiaoyu doesn't know the word 螃蟹 (crab) so he describes it. This is a great example of circumlocution (迂回表达) — a useful survival strategy when you don't know a word.
