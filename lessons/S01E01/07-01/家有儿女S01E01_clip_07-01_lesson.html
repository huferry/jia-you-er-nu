WordPinyinExplanation
那是
colloq.		nà shì'Of course! / Naturally!' — a spoken interjection of agreement, often a bit smug: 'well, obviously'. Not the literal 'that is' here.
世纪
HSK4		shìjì'Century'. 二十一世纪 = 21st century. Compare 年代 (decade, e.g. 八十年代 = the '80s).
城市
HSK3		chéngshì'City'. 城市女性 = urban women. Opposite: 农村 (countryside).
仨
colloq.		sā'Three (of something)' = 三个, northern colloquial. Like 俩 (liǎ = 两个), it already contains the measure word, so never say ×仨个.
概率
HSK6		gàilǜ'Probability, odds'. 率 is lǜ here (as in 效率 xiàolǜ), not shuài. A semi-technical word used humorously in this line.
中头奖
idiom		zhòng tóujiǎng'Win the top prize / hit the jackpot'. 中 is 4th tone (zhòng) = to hit a target, as in 中奖 (win a prize), 猜中 (guess right) — not zhōng (middle).
差不多
HSK4		chàbuduō'About the same, almost'. A跟B差不多 = A is about the same as B. 不 is light/neutral here.
比…多了
grammar		bǐ … duō leComparison 'much more…': A比B + adj + 多了. 她的床比我的舒服多了 = her bed is much more comfortable than mine. You can't use 很 here (×比我的很舒服).
舒服
HSK3		shūfu'Comfortable' — 服 is toneless (fu). Also 'feeling well': 我不舒服 = I'm not feeling well.
弄乱
HSK5 (弄)		nòngluàn'To mess up' — 弄 (do/make, vague all-purpose verb) + 乱 (messy) as result. Pattern 把X(都)给弄乱了 = 'went and messed X up' (给 adds a sense of unwanted outcome).
