HSK 3+ Vocabulary

WordPinyinExplanation
难道
HSK4		nán dàoDon't tell me…; could it be that…; is it really the case that…? Rhetorical question marker — the speaker expects the answer to be "no." 你们难道不想问点什么吗 = don't you guys even want to ask me anything? Signals mild indignation or disbelief. Compare 莫非 (more literary), 该不会 (more tentative).
熟悉
HSK4		shú xiTo be familiar with; to get to know. 熟悉熟悉 = get (yourself) familiar (reduplication softens and casualizes). 你应该熟悉熟悉这新家的周边环境 = you should get to know the area around your new home. As an adjective: 我对这里很熟悉 = I'm very familiar with this place. The reduplication V一V or VV implies a light, brief action.
周边环境
HSK5		zhōu biān huán jìngSurrounding environment, the area around (a place). 周边 = surrounding, peripheral; 环境 = environment, surroundings. Used here about the neighborhood around the new home. Common collocations: 周边设施 (nearby facilities), 生态环境 (ecological environment), 学习环境 (learning environment).
小区
colloquial		xiǎo qūResidential compound, housing estate. The standard word for a gated or semi-gated apartment/housing community in Chinese cities. 咱们那小区 = our compound / our estate. Common in everyday speech: 小区里面 (inside the compound), 小区门口 (at the compound gate). Not to be confused with 社区 (community — broader term).
待遇
HSK5		dài yùTreatment, benefits, perks. 这种待遇 = this kind of treatment. Here the boys are jealously noting that Xiaoxue is being handled with kid gloves. 待遇 also refers to salary/compensation in work contexts: 薪资待遇 (salary and benefits). The irony in this scene: the boys want the VIP treatment, but dad's response is just 耐心 宽大为怀.
享受
HSK4		xiǎng shòuTo enjoy; to have the benefit of. 享受这种待遇 = enjoy this kind of treatment/perk. As a noun: 这是一种享受 = this is a pleasure/luxury. Common collocations: 享受生活 (enjoy life), 享受权利 (enjoy rights/privileges). Slightly ironic here — the boys use an elevated word to mock-protest the unequal treatment.
