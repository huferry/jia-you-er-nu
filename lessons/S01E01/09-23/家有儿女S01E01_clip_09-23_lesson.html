HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
后妈
colloquial		hòumā饿了给水喝，那叫后妈 = "giving water when hungry — that's what a stepmother would do." In Chinese folk culture 后妈 (stepmother) is the archetype of heartless caregiving. Used here as a biting hyperbole, not a literal accusation.
趁着
HSK5		chènzheTo seize/take advantage of a time or situation. Pattern: 趁着 + condition + action. 趁着姐姐没来 = while sister hasn't arrived yet. Related idiom: 趁热打铁 (strike while the iron is hot).
嘱咐
HSK5		zhǔfùTo give earnest, repeated instructions. More heartfelt than 告诉 — implies the speaker really cares. Common: 临走前嘱咐 (give instructions just before leaving). 再嘱咐你两句 = let me remind you of a couple more things.
脑供血不足
idiom		nǎo gōng xuè bù zúLiterally "insufficient blood supply to the brain" — a real medical term (cerebral ischemia). Used here as comic self-deprecation: 我已经脑供血不足了 = my brain is already oxygen-starved, don't expect me to remember things.
文静
HSK5		wénjìngQuiet, gentle, refined (of character). Almost always applied to girls/women. Contrast with 活泼 (lively, outgoing). In Chinese parenting rhetoric, 文静 is high praise for a daughter.
乖乖女
colloquial		guāiguāi nǚA well-behaved, obedient "good girl". 乖 (guāi) means docile and compliant; doubling it adds an affectionate, cutesy tone. Boy equivalent: 乖宝宝. A 乖乖女 causes no trouble and does what she's told — which is exactly what the mom is preparing 小雨 to expect.
