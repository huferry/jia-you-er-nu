HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
起码
HSK4		qǐ mǎAt least, at the minimum. 起码她没跑回爷爷家去 = at least she didn't run off to grandpa's. Useful for drawing a minimal positive conclusion from a bad situation. Compare 至少 (zhì shǎo) — same meaning, slightly more formal. 起码 is very natural in spoken Mandarin.
凭什么
colloquial		píng shén meOn what grounds? Why should (I/she/he)? Expresses indignation — the speaker finds something unjustified. 凭 = based on, relying on. 凭什么呀 = what right does she have?! / why on earth would she?! Very common in spoken Chinese to push back against something you feel is unwarranted.
委屈
HSK5		wěi quTo feel wronged, aggrieved; a grievance. 受委屈 = to suffer a grievance / to be wronged. 这孩子是怕受委屈 = this child is afraid of being hurt/slighted. As a verb: 委屈你了 = I've put you in a difficult position / sorry for making things hard for you. Very common in family and emotional contexts.
先发制人
HSK6/idiom		xiān fā zhì rénStrike first to gain the upper hand; preemptive action. Chengyu: 先 = first, 发 = launch, 制 = control, 人 = others. Originally a military/strategy term. Here 夏东海 uses it to psychologically explain Xiaoxue's hostile behavior: she attacks first so she can't be hurt first.
管
HSK3		guǎnTo look after, to be in charge of, to manage, to care for. 没怎么管她 = didn't really look after her / didn't manage her well. 管 has a range: 管孩子 (discipline/raise a child), 管事 (handle affairs), 不管 (regardless of / to not care). Here carries a note of parental guilt.
可怜
HSK4		kě liánPitiful, poor (evoking sympathy). 孩子挺可怜的 = the kid is quite pitiful / the poor thing. 挺…的 is a mild intensifier: pretty, rather. 可怜 can be used sympathetically (poor child) or condescendingly. Here 夏东海 says it with genuine empathy about Xiaoxue's difficult situation growing up without close parental oversight.
