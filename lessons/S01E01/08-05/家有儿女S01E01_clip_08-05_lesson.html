HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
预备
HSK6		yùbèi'Get ready': 预备 — 开始! = 'Ready — go!' (the classic start command, like 预备 — 跑!). Close to 准备 but sounds more like a formal countdown.
闹
HSK4		nào'To make a racket, horse around'. 这么闹 = carry on like this. Also 闹钟 (alarm clock), 热闹 (lively).
非得…不可
grammar		fēiděi … bùkě'Will definitely / is bound to…' (an inevitable, usually bad result): 非得把姐姐吓着不可 = it's bound to scare her. The next line has the variant 非得…不行. 得 is děi.
吓着
HSK5 (吓)		xiàzháo'To give someone a fright'. 着 is zháo here — a result complement meaning 'actually affected' (also 睡着 shuìzháo, 找着). 把X吓着 = scare X.
而且
HSK3		érqiě'And also, moreover' — adds a further point. Often paired: 不但…而且… (not only… but also…).
不许
HSK4		bùxǔ'Not allowed to' — a blunt parental ban: 吃完饭不许演. Stronger than 不要 or 别.
阑尾炎
noun		lánwěiyán'Appendicitis'. Old folk belief: running and jumping right after a meal can bring it on — that's the joke. 炎 = inflammation (as in 肺炎 pneumonia).
丰盛
HSK6		fēngshèng'Rich, sumptuous' — almost always about a meal: 丰盛的晚餐/午餐. Compare 丰富 (abundant, rich — of content, experience, etc.).
晚餐
HSK4		wǎncān'Dinner' — slightly more formal than 晚饭. 一桌…晚餐 = a whole tableful of dinner (桌 used as a measure word).
