HSK 3+ Vocabulary

Words from the text above that are HSK level 3 or higher (levels approximate).
WordPinyinExplanation
秃小子
colloq.		tū xiǎoziLiterally 'bald little kid' — affectionate northern slang for a (scruffy, rough-and-tumble) little boy. Contrast 闺女 in the next line: boy vs. girl. 子 is toneless (zi).
闺女
colloq.		guīnǚ'Daughter / girl' — warm, colloquial northern word. 一大闺女 = 一(个)大闺女, 'a big (grown-up) girl'; the measure word 个 is often dropped after 一 in fast speech.
从来没
grammar		cónglái méi'Never (ever) have…': 从来 + 没/不 + verb (+过). 我从来没想过 = 'I never once thought'. 从来 is almost always used with a negative.
保证
HSK4		bǎozhèng'To guarantee, promise'. 我保证 is a strong spoken promise — stronger than 我答应 (I agree to). Also a noun: 质量保证 (quality guarantee).
感觉
HSK4		gǎnjuéAs a noun 'feeling/sense' (这种感觉 = this feeling); also a verb 'to feel' (我感觉很好). Close to 觉得, but 感觉 leans more toward sensations and impressions.
文静
HSK6		wénjìng'Gentle and quiet' — refined, calm, not rowdy; used almost exclusively for girls/women. Contrast 安静 (quiet, of a place or a moment).
礼貌 / 教养
HSK4 / HSK6		lǐmào / jiàoyǎng懂礼貌 = 'has good manners' (lit. 'understands courtesy'); 有教养 = 'well brought up, cultured'. 教 is 4th tone (jiào) here, not jiāo (to teach).
典型
HSK5		diǎnxíng'Typical, classic (example)': 典型的乖乖女 = 'a textbook good girl'. More emphatic than 一般 (ordinary) — it's the perfect representative of a type.
乖乖女
idiom		guāiguāi nǚ'Good girl' — obedient, well-behaved daughter. 乖 = well-behaved (said of children/pets); 真乖! = 'Good boy/girl!'
尖子生
colloq.		jiānzishēng'Top student' — 尖子 = the tip/the best of a group. Synonym: 优等生, 学霸 (newer slang).
眼皮底下
idiom		yǎnpí dǐxia'Right under one's nose' (lit. 'under the eyelids'). 在X的眼皮底下 = right in front of X, where X can't miss it.
树立 / 榜样
HSK6 / HSK5		shùlì / bǎngyàng树立榜样 = 'set an example' — a fixed collocation. 树立 also goes with 形象 (image), 信心 (confidence). 活榜样 = 'a living role model'.
